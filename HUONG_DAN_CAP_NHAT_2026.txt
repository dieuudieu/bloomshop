BLOOMSHOP (3) - CAP NHAT TON KHO & TRA HANG - MVC OOP

1. Giai nen toan bo ZIP vao C:\xampp\htdocs\bloomshop\ (hoac ten folder ban muon).
2. File model/config.php co local DB bloomshop / root / mat khau rong.
   Neu ten DB khac, sua config.php.
3. Neu ban da import bloomshop.sql TU ZIP NAY, cac cot so_luong, stock_deducted,
   inventory_restored, voucher_restored, return_reason, return_requested_at
   va cac trang thai tra hang DA TON TAI. KHONG import lai SQL neu DB dang co du lieu.
4. Vao Admin -> Quan ly san pham -> Sua san pham, nhap ton kho thuc te.
   San pham cu co the dang ton 0.
5. Test: them vao gio, dat hang, kiem tra ton kho giam; huy don cho xac nhan
   kiem tra ton kho tang va voucher hoan luot.
6. Test tra hang: tai khoan khach vao Lich su don hang, don da giao/thanh cong,
   nhap ly do va gui yeu cau. Admin -> Quan ly don hang -> Duyet/Tu choi.
7. Tra hang la tra TOAN BO don. Duyet tra hang se hoan ton kho va luot voucher.
   KHONG tu dong hoan tien qua ngan hang/vi dien tu.
8. Bao ve: danh muc con san pham khong xoa duoc; san pham da co chi tiet don
   khong xoa duoc. Da co logic chan trong model cua source goc.
9. Don hang cu co stock_deducted=0 se KHONG duoc hoan ton kho khi tra,
   de tranh cong them ton kho cho don chua tung tru kho.
10. Sao luu DATABASE va SOURCE truoc khi dua len hosting. Test tren XAMPP truoc.

CHU Y: SQL dump bloomshop.sql trong source co the chua du lieu tai khoan/khach hang
cua ban; khong chia se file do cong khai. Khong dua .git len hosting.
